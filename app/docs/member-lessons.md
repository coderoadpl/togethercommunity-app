# Member lessons

Open a lesson from the course curriculum to read its content and use the lesson
actions to mark it complete or continue to the next lesson.

Long text starts in a centred, clipped reading preview with a bottom fade.
Visible text remains selectable, links follow their destinations, and images
remain interactive. Tab focus inside the text expands its block automatically.
Use **Show all** to read the full block and **Collapse text** to return to the
preview. Heading deep links expand their block before scrolling. Enable
**Expand lesson text automatically** in **Account → Playback** to start expanded;
the preference is remembered in this browser. See [Reading lesson text](lessons.md).

Images in lesson content open in a lightbox. Click or tap an image, or focus it
with Tab and press Enter or Space, to enlarge it. Screen readers announce an
image by its alternative text followed by "Enlarge image"; an image without
alternative text is announced as "Enlarge image". The image sits on a dark
backdrop in both themes, on a white backing so transparent images stay readable,
and reaches the screen edges on a phone. An SVG fills the available space; a
raster image is shown at most at its natural size. A figure caption appears in
light text underneath; an image without a figure caption shows no caption. The
lightbox is named after the alternative text, then the figure caption.

Use the Close button in the top corner, press Escape, or click or tap anywhere
outside the image to return to the lesson; a click on the image or its caption
keeps the lightbox open. Keyboard focus returns to the image. The lightbox covers the lesson action bar and
navigation tabs and keeps the page from scrolling while open. Images inside
links follow their links.
