# Tenant settings

Video autoplay is controlled by the creator in **Studio → Settings → Company → Video playback**. The tenant default applies when member overrides are disabled or a member has not chosen a preference; when overrides are enabled, members can choose autoplay from their account page. The CLI exposes the same policy through `tenant settings` and `tenant settings-set --video-autoplay-default <true|false> --member-video-autoplay-override <true|false>`.

Tenant branding has two accents in **Studio → Settings → Branding**: `accentColor` for the dark scheme and optional `accentLight` for the light scheme. Leaving the light accent empty derives a darker shade of the same hue with at least 4.5:1 text contrast (also exceeding the 3:1 control requirement) on light page and card backgrounds. The form shows both schemes and the derived value before saving; links, controls and purchase buttons follow the active scheme. The CLI shows both values with `tenant settings` and updates them with `tenant settings-set --accent-color '#F5C842' --accent-light '#786000'`; `--clear-accent-light` restores automatic derivation, and `--clear-accent-color` removes the dark-scheme accent.
